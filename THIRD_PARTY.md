## Acknowledgements

The idea to rebuild VIEW, and the lead to its documents, came from a thread by NB ([@Noahbolanowski](https://x.com/Noahbolanowski/status/2104604685810151481)) on X, 28 September 2026. The thread photographs the MSC IN 69-FM-197 and TN D-6853 covers and plots, and recounts VIEW's role in Apollo 13. This repository uses the NTRS scans of those documents, not the thread's photographs.
