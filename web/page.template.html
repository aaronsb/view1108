A reconstruction of VIEW, the Manned Spacecraft Center program that drew Apollo window views on a UNIVAC 1108 (FORTRAN V, output photographed from a CRT onto microfilm: NASA TN D-6853, p. 3). We know of no surviving source code; the geometry here is our own FORTRAN and this page only films it.

G. B. Roush, Computation and Analysis Division, MSC, is credited with developing the major analytical tool (MSC IN 69-FM-197). Sources: NASA TN D-6853 (Hyle and Lunde, 1972); MSC IN 69-FM-197. Code MIT; data, fonts and three.js under their own licences: THIRD_PARTY.md.
