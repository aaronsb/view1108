VIEW-1108

A reconstruction of VIEW, the Manned Spacecraft Center program that drew Apollo window views on a UNIVAC 1108 (FORTRAN V, output photographed from a CRT onto microfilm: NASA TN D-6853, p. 3). We know of no surviving source code; the geometry here is our own FORTRAN and this page only films it.

g.e.t.
Jump (Live)
Drag: look around. Wheel / pinch: field of view. Arrows: yaw, pitch. Q/E: roll. +/-: field of view. Space: pause. [ ]: speed. R: reset. 1-5: scene. J: film jitter. D: dust. F: 16 fps film rate. T: beam trace. L: copy link. B: bloom. C: star catalog. Any input leaves Attract or Tour.
G. B. Roush, Computation and Analysis Division, MSC, is credited with developing the major analytical tool (MSC IN 69-FM-197). Sources: NASA TN D-6853 (Hyle and Lunde, 1972); MSC IN 69-FM-197.
